Manage graphs.

Bring a Desmos graph into Room310. Its preview becomes the cover automatically.

How to add a Desmos graph Start here ↗
  1. Open your graph in the Desmos Graphing Calculator and click Share.
  2. Choose Save and Share This Graph for a link that follows your saved edits, or Share a Snapshot for a fixed version.
  3. Copy the share link. In Room310, click Add graph, paste the link, then click Import graph. Desmos's iframe embed code also works—no coding required.
  4. Add a title and description. Keep the automatic picture of your graph, or choose Custom image to upload a cover.
  5. Choose Published and save. Your card opens a graph page on Room310. Choose Draft to keep it private while you work.

After changing a saved graph in Desmos, save it there and re-import its link here to refresh the cover. Embedded graphs still need access to Desmos. Desmos sharing help ↗
